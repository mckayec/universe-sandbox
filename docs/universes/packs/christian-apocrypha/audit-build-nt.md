## Findings

- Existing APB-001 also affects line 1550, people codex “Eubula”, which repeats “Agrippinus or Pompeius”. Correct it together with event line 601.
- Medium, NT-01: lines 639, 1139, 1577 (nta-at-initiation; The house of Iuzanes and Mnesara; Iuzanes and Mnesara) generalize healing to both spouses. In [Acts of Thomas 151,154–158](https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/actsthomas.html), Mnesara is ill and recovers; both spouses receive initiation. Suggested wording: “Mnesara is healed; she and Iuzanes are initiated at their house.” The research cluster's compressed “healing and initiation” should not become plural physical healing.
- Low, NT-02: line 639 “Narcia is ... not anyone’s mother”, and line 1575 “A nurse, not a mother”, exceed the research's no-biological-parent-inference rule. [Acts of Thomas 120–121](https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/actsthomas.html) identifies a nurse and preserves maternal address. Suggested wording: “Mygdonia's nurse; the maternal address does not establish biological parentage.” Do not assert that she has no children or cannot also be a mother.
- Low, NT-03: line 590 nta-pt-ephesus says “Known only from quotations by Hippolytus and Nicephorus”. The [selected James VIII section](https://www.earlychristianwritings.com/text/actspaul.html) also explicitly supplies an Acts of Titus sentence. Remove “only” or name all three witnesses. This concerns selected-edition evidence wording, not a demand to narrate every quotation.
