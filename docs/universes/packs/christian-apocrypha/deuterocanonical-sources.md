# Deuterocanonical core: sources, witnesses and access

Read date:2026-10-26. The [unit ledger](deuterocanonical-coverage.csv) is the authoritative per-unit list of successful URLs, edition, actual reading extent, extracted material and limits. URLs with semicolons identify multiple witnesses/parts, not one URL. Entries are paraphrased; no copyrighted source bodies or source images are included. Reading evidence is the retained passage-specific notes and actual tool responses, not a claim that a file hash proves reading. No immutable raw-source archive is supplied.

## Editions and limits

**USCCB / NABRE.** Official Catholic publisher-hosted biblical text and editorial notes. The body establishes what this translation says; notes establish the editors' interpretation, not independent archaeological verification. Copyright notices do not grant permission to redistribute the entire translation or apparatus. We provide short original paraphrases and direct citations only. Parent corpus acceptance must preserve alternate witnesses below rather than silently presenting them as NABRE.

**Revised Version with Apocrypha1895 / eBible.** Pages identify this edition and mark the text public domain. Used where USCCB bodies failed; the exact page is recorded. This is a distinct translation/versification witness. RV Tobit5–6 and2:10 are not silently spliced into a claim of uniform NABRE/long-recension reading. Names Tobias/Azarias/Achiacharus correspond in context to Tobiah/Azariah/Ahiqar, but source spelling remains traceable. Same-name distant genealogical figures are not merged automatically.

**Douay-Rheims1899 American Edition / Bible Gateway.** Opened Esther13 and15; both pages identify their edition and public-domain status. Used only for inaccessible Esther decree/prayer/intercession portions and audience comparison. DRA13:6 says14Adar; RV counterdecree16:20 says13Adar. These are not harmonized. The original Latin/Vulgate or underlying Greek was not independently collated.

## Core body witness substitutions

- Tobit: NABRE full bodies1,7–14. RV full2–6; partial NABRE2:1–4 and4:1–6 additionally inspected before subsequent fetch403. NABRE3,5,6 failed; no claim its unread variants were compared. RV2:10 first-person Elymais wording has a third-person variant; RV6:5/9 Ecbatana/Rages tension remains in records.
- Judith: NABRE1,2,4,5,9–12,16; RV3,6–8,13–15 after USCCB403. All16 chapter bodies were read in one named witness; NABRE comparison is incomplete.
- Baruch: NABRE2,3,5,6; RV1,4 after USCCB403. Letter of Jeremiah is one unit here (Baruch6), not double-counted as a seventh chapter.
- Wisdom: NABRE except1,5,10,19, read in RV. Wisdom introduction unavailable; no composition-date/city assertion made.
- Sirach: NABRE except4,22,34,37,38,44,46,51, read in RV; translator prologue read on RV SIR01 before chapter1. Prologue is a separately counted accompanying unit, not an extra canonical chapter. Sirach36 displaced/absent verse numbers are noted, not treated as missed reader work.
- Esther additions: USCCB intro opened; its A–F body links failed. A=RV11:2–12:6; B=DRA13:1–7 plus15:1–3 injunction; C=DRA13:8–18 plusRV14:1–19; D=RV15:1–16 (DRA15:4–19 compared); E=RV16:1–24; F=RV10:4–13 plus11:1colophon. This is an explicitly cross-edition section pass, not a uniform NABRE read. RV13 and attempted WEB13 also inaccessible, hence DRA witness.
- Daniel additions: NABRE14; RV Susanna1:1–64 and Song1:1–68 after USCCB13/3 failed. Song's omitted verse and variant notes were read. The printed numbering is not68 identical consecutive verses aligned mechanically to NABRE3:24–90.

Specific failures are access limitations of these requests, not proof the works cannot be accessed anywhere. Alternate bodies close substantive reading gaps while leaving edition comparison open. All coordinates remain unresearched; original-language recension comparison and independent historical corroboration are not claimed.

## Failed access and scope exclusions

https://bible.usccb.org/bible/1maccabees/0 and https://bible.usccb.org/bible/2maccabees/0 returned HTTP403. They are attempted sources, not claim-bearing sources. No assertion of an introduction reading, NABRE comparison, or critical Greek-manuscript collation is made. No RV chapter was inaccessible.

The RV notes are evidence for what this edition flags, not a replacement for modern textual scholarship. In particular, the ledger preserves Gazara/Gaza, Samaria/Marisa, Caspin/Gephyrun, Dioscorinthius and uncertain personal-name readings. Modern calendar conversion and exact site identification remain excluded pending separately inspected evidence.
