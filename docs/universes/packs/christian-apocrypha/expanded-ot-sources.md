### Meqabyan access update: provisional Iyaric witness

This2026-10-26 discovery supersedes “no accessible primary body” for2/3Meqabyan. It does **not** establish translation reliability. The introduction signs “RFI,” calls the translation Iyaric, and asks for help with Amharic words at1Meqabyan8:1,8:29,9:2,19:1,30:14,35:7. Translator identity, base edition, date and reuse permission remain unverified. No official Church authorization is established; no full text is republished.
