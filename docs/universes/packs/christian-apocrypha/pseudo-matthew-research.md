# Gospel of Pseudo-Matthew: selected expanded Walker text

2026-10-26. Work key `pseudo-matthew-walker`; chapter gates follow the displayed numbered chapters. Root research assigns the numeric `first` work mapping.

## Scope, identity and sources

**42/42 displayed chapters plus three prefatory units read.** This is a bounded overview of the expanded text in Alexander Walker's translation, not a claim that all 42 chapters formed one original composition. The CSV contains 45 rows: request letter, reply, alternative attributed letter, and chapters 1–42. No chapter is access-blocked.

**PM1:** [New Advent, Gospel of Pseudo-Matthew](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/0848.htm), entire displayed body opened in successive windows. Page credits Alexander Walker, *Ante-Nicene Fathers* 8 (1886), edited by Roberts, Donaldson and Coxe; revised for New Advent by Kevin Knight. Underlying nineteenth-century translation and modern editorial presentation are distinct. All proposed copy here is original paraphrase; no full-text redistribution or blanket license for modern revisions is asserted.

**PM2:** [Brandon W. Hawk, NASSCAL, Gospel of Pseudo-Matthew](https://www.nasscal.com/e-clavis-christian-apocrypha/gospel-of-pseudo-matthew/), current April 2023. Identifies ECCA 545/CANT 51 and a Latin work heavily adapting the *Protevangelium of James*. It distinguishes later *pars altera* additions from an early Latin *Infancy Gospel of Thomas* and further accretions from another Latin translation, and catalogs differing manuscript families. Thus this selected expanded display must not be treated as a uniform early core. [PM2] (C-scholarship). The 1–24 / 25 / 26–42 ledger division describes the displayed narrative's Mary/Egypt sequence, return bridge and childhood continuation; it is not a new critical reconstruction of every manuscript boundary.

The purported Matthew/Hebrew/Jerome authorship and translation chain belongs to the prefatory fiction/claims. It is not authenticated correspondence or evidence that Matthew wrote this text. Exact composition dating and critical manuscript-family placement of every Walker paragraph remain unverified here. No tradition's canonical status is inferred from the title or transmission.

All narrative rows are `(C-text)`: the source says this, not independent historical proof. Work-specific miracles, ages, family relationships and topography must retain source attribution. The narrative's judgments about Jewish interlocutors or Egyptian worshippers must not become the atlas's own judgment of those peoples.

Narrative ages and intervals remain strings, not BCE/CE dates: Mary three and fourteen (4,8); Jesus entering fourth year (26), scarcely five (30), six (33), eight (35); cave-to-stable on day three (14), entry into Bethlehem day six (15), magi after the second year (16). These are this text's chronology and need not be harmonized with other infancy accounts. [PM1] (C-text).

## Coverage limits and validation

The body and all displayed prefatory material are covered, including the later childhood continuation. Not covered: manuscript collation, all Latin textual families, historical verification of each episode, cult-site identifications, or full parallel comparison with James/Thomas. A literary relation cited from scholarship is not a claim that every parallel wording was independently collated.

Validated 45 CSV rows (3 prefatory + 42 unique numbered chapters), 42 event/topic cards, 14 places, 7 journeys, 8 codex entries and 2 sources. No code changes, commits or publication. Evidence establishes a usable selected-text overview, not final canon sign-off.
