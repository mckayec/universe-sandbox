# Marian supplement: Greek Pseudo-John Dormition

Research date: 2026-10-26. Work key `dormition-pseudo-john-greek`; root mapping supplies numeric `first`. Gate each fact by local editorial unit MU01–MU12 below. These are research navigation units, **not ancient chapter numbers**.

## Identity and finite coverage

The primary text is the **first work only** on New Advent's “Apocryphal Works on the Assumption of Mary”: the account attributed to John the Theologian, from its opening prayer at Jesus' tomb through its first concluding Amen, immediately before “The Passing of Mary / First Latin Form.” All of that work was opened and read. The following First and Second Latin Forms are different texts and are not silently joined to it. [M1] (C-text).

NASSCAL identifies this as *Dormition of the Virgin by the Apostle John*, ECCA 458 / Greek CANT 101, and records Tischendorf's Greek edition based on five manuscripts, alongside versions in other languages. Its bibliography and identification are scholarly evidence; neither pseudonymous first-person narration nor an apostolic title establishes apostolic authorship. [M2] (C-bibliography). This deliverable is not Transitus Latin W and does not claim to cover its variable ending. It also excludes the Six-Books tradition, other Greek/Latin/Ethiopic recensions, and Pseudo-Matthew. No precise composition date is asserted without further critical evidence.

**Coverage:** 12/12 editorial narrative units span the complete selected account; 12 events, 7 selected place records, 4 journey records, 4 codex entries. This completes one usable Marian narrative overview, not all Marian literature or a denomination's doctrine/canon review.

## Codex and recension safeguards

| ID | Exact short copy | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| MC01 | The narrator presents himself as John, summoned to Mary from Ephesus. | M1 MU03. Literary attribution, not verified eyewitness authorship. |
| MC02 | Some apostles are temporarily raised to attend Mary; the text distinguishes this from the general resurrection. | M1 MU04. |
| MC03 | Mary asks her son to grant mercy to those invoking her name. | M1 MU09. Textual prayer, not a universal Christian teaching claim. |
| MC04 | Jephonias attacks the funeral couch and receives restoration through Peter's words. | M1 MU11. No generalized claim about Jews. |

Thomas is present before Mary's departure in this Greek account. Do not import the late-Thomas/girdle episode from the next Latin work. Peter comes from Rome here; do not replace that with the adjacent Latin form's Antioch. The three attendants here are unnamed; do not import Sepphora, Abigea and Zaël from the next account. Mary's body is said to reach **paradise**, while her soul is promised the heavens; preserve that distinction instead of flattening different recensions into a single universal account. [M1 MU02, MU04–MU05, MU09–MU12] (C-text).

Narrative time: preparation day at the opening, five days of praise after transfer to Jerusalem, Lord's-day departure, and three days of angelic sound at the tomb. No absolute year is assigned. The account's invocation of Tiberius in the opposition episode is retained only as an internal detail, not reconciled into a historical timeline. [M1 MU01, MU07, MU09, MU12] (C-text).
