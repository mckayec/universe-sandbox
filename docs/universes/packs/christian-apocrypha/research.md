## 1. Meta

**Design:** id `christian-apocrypha`; title “Biblical Apocrypha and Related Traditions”; TrackA, Earth-historical layer plus nonspatial text library. Description: “Explore additional biblical and early Christian writings through their distinct texts, settings and reception traditions.” Theme: parchment, deep blue, original line diagrams; no copied manuscript art.

Works are identified in the dossiers by stable work/recension keys. The [global navigation mapping](work-gates.csv) and [integration contract](integration-contract.md) define 39 work-family slots; these are not a universal canonical ordering. Narrative, composition and edition dates must occupy separate fields. Unknown composition dates remain unknown, not guessed from an attributed apostolic author.

Rights notice: “Independent educational research. Ancient texts, modern translations and manuscript images have different rights and provenance. Short original summaries identify the editions consulted.” Rights context belongs to each source entry; no blanket claim that every hosted translation is public domain.

## 2. Structure

Use source/tradition filters: deuterocanonical core; expanded OT collections; related pseudepigrapha; NT-related gospels/acts/apocalypses; Marian and Pilate traditions. These are browsing groups, not mutually exclusive religious truth categories. Institutional canon descriptions are in AP-C01–06 of [corpus-and-method.md](corpus-and-method.md).

Earth is the geographic parent for genuinely located places. Visionary heavens, paradise and underworlds are text/schematic settings with no Earth coordinate. The [geography reuse contract](geography-reuse.md) provides five already researched representative anchors with exact prior artifact revisions. It does not locate the narrated miracles or authenticate the stories.

## 3. Places

Dossier place registers supply names, source passages and narrative associations. Keep work-specific views even when neutral city identities are shared. Bethlehem is not every proposed nativity cave; Jerusalem is not an exact tribunal/tomb; Babylon is not Babylonia. Bethulia, Mamilch and unresolved sites remain unpinned. Existing labels cannot transfer dates, miracles or territorial claims between works.

Selected usable examples are PG-P01Mamilch, PL-P01Hades and PL-P02Paradise in the Pilate dossier, all explicitly nonspatial. Other dossier registers are being consolidated; there is no completed global place-count claim.

## 4. Events

Each proposed event must name work, chapter/section, characters, setting and source-specific time. PG-E01–03 and PL-E01 in the Pilate dossier are text events, not authenticated Roman records. The manuscript's narrative synchronisms must remain separate from scholarship about composition.

Do not merge distinct martyrdom traditions, competing infancy accounts, different dates or different endings into one harmonized timeline. Exact source dates may be displayed as text without a numeric historical year. The PL12 chronology's listed intervals total5430 although the witness asserts5500; both are recorded with the arithmetic explicitly identified as an integrator calculation.

## 5. Journeys

Use the actor and ordered stages actually stated. PG-J01Joseph's return and PG-J02witness travel are narrative sequences; PL-J01ascent from Hades and PL-J02resurrected movement are not surveyed routes. A copied traditional journey can be an explanatory card without a globe polyline. Miraculous compression, visionary ascent and unknown intermediate sites remain visible limits.

## 6. Codex

Reference entries distinguish canon reception, attributed author, surviving witness, narrator, religious teaching, scholarly interpretation and geographic proposal. Examples include the AP-C reception entries, PG-C01–02named narrative figures and PL-C01–02chronological/documentary claims. Source registers and section ledgers are accessible to the builder; bibliography membership alone never establishes body access.

Typed relationships must distinguish kinship within a story, a character's testimony, textual adaptation and scholarly manuscript relationship. Mary/Thomas/Peter/Paul titles do not automatically identify a work's historical author. Repeated biblical names require explicit alias review.

## 8. TrackA additions

The display must identify **text**, **community reception**, **scholarship** and **geographic evidence** separately. Canon lists are attributed to their issuing traditions. A late narrative's theological accusation is not a factual description of a population; preserve speaker attribution and avoid collective guilt. An ancient work's authoritative status for one community does not prove a proposed modern site, and a scholarly date does not adjudicate religious truth.

No single harmonized canon, reconstructed exact ancient road, complete original-language collation or historical verification of visionary geography is claimed. Research remains owned by ChatGPT until the explicitly defined acceptance work is complete.
