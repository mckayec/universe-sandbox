## Included and separate material

Target: fourteen numbered novels plus the expanded prequel novel New Spring [WOS1] (C). The prequel's research/navigation slot is15, separate from main-series1–14; this is a conservative UI choice, not a claim that its events happen last. Narrative time and reader disclosure are independent.

River of Souls is an author-confirmed canonical companion sequence; include it as a separately gated supplement only after reading an identified body. A Fire in the Ways is explicitly noncanonical in Sanderson's account and must not enter the main continuity [WOS2] (C). Neither body has been read here. Standalone prologue sales, split editions and adaptations must not be counted as extra novels without checking their content. The television and graphic-novel continuities remain separate and unused.

The Companion is useful editorial evidence but warns that notes can diverge from published novels [WOS3] (C). Novel passages take priority for the main book atlas; retain a discrepancy rather than silently harmonizing author notes. The World of Robert Jordan's The Wheel of Time and other reference titles are not read-body evidence merely because the publisher lists them.

## Scope of current work

This is a partial, source-backed checkpoint, not a whole-series handoff. Official primary excerpts support compact candidate records. No full novel has been read in this task. Full-text access, chapter enumeration, first-appearance verification, complete journeys, map relationships and chronology remain open. An unread chapter is never labelled “no atlas material.”

Use stable book gates1–14 and a separate optional prequel slot15. A later-book excerpt supplies a **known-by** gate, not proof that a name or fact first appeared there. Until earlier books are checked, conservative late disclosure is permitted for research but cannot be described as completed earliest-appearance auditing. Current engine gates by book; chapter locators are provenance and ordering, not a promised chapter slider.

Do not identify the opening rider, disclose later family revelations, or attach later political borders to the first-book village description from memory. Full novel contents and lawful longer excerpts are needed to close those gaps. Reader-facing dates stay unknown unless a passage establishes them; edition publication dates are not story dates.

## Geography and rights

Track B uses an original schematic world map, not real Earth latitude/longitude. `wot-world` is a neutral implementation ID, not a canonical planet name. Relative descriptions may be sourced without inventing scale or continent outlines. The world-only engine proposal does not authorize tracing published maps or importing official art. No numeric coordinates are asserted at this checkpoint. Nonspatial reference records preserve usable facts until geography is researched.
