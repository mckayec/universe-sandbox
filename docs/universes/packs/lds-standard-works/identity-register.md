2026-10-26. This registry gives global dispositions for prominent collisions across the core, inventories and chapter files. It is an integration contract, not a claim that every proper noun has been atomized. Existing uppercase keys remain research references; one builder entity may be referenced by several source-specific chapter records. Never emit an extra map site merely because a later chapter mentions an existing place.

All place mentions in quotations, visions, prophecies, parables and accusations must retain that mode. A candidate relationship is typed as parent, spouse, sibling, companion, opponent, office holder, record custodian or source interpretation only when the cited passage supports that type. Shared names are not proof of kinship; succession is not proof of parentage; a missionary assignment is not proof of completed travel.
