## Evidence and review-depth rules

- `inspected`: every numbered verse in the chapter was read in the available KJV transcription and/or official Church page, followed by the recorded atlas disposition. This means atlas screening, not independent historical corroboration or manuscript comparison.
- `text-partial`: named-place passages and/or selected official passages were read, but a full remaining-verse pass is still required. This is **not** an approved whole-chapter exclusion.
- `pending`: file retrieved, but no substantive passage review recorded yet; the draft is a research candidate, not source-verified output.
- `(C)` on a chapter row confirms the attributed textual summary from inspected text. `(L)` means the proposed summary still needs a full claim-by-claim check and is **held from this handoff's build set**, even though the general template ordinarily permits likely facts. Geography-only matches do not prove all event/person details in the row.
- Official links in the chapter table are reference targets; an `O` in the evidence column means the official page was actually opened in this continuation. `T` means the separately disclosed third-party KJV transcription was used. A link's existence does not count as source access.
- All rows have `layer:bible`, `first:2`, `parent:earth` for geographical subjects; visionary/conceptual material has no physical parent coordinate. The existing OT/NT navigation does not implement chapter-level reading gates. Per-chapter keys are provenance, not a new supported engine field.
- All scripture facts are **text claims**. No miracle is presented as archaeological proof. No new modern coordinate or absolute historical date is approved here. Source-specific chronology, variant narrative sequences, rhetorical imagery and hypothetical journeys remain separate.

## Cross-source integration and correction rules

1. Reuse existing `P06` Nazareth, `P08` Jerusalem, `P20` Mount of Olives and `AP08` Capernaum/`AP09` Sea of Galilee rather than creating duplicates. John6:1 explicitly equates Galilee/Tiberias; Luke5 calls the lake Gennesaret. A region named Gennesaret is not the same feature type as the lake.
2. Keep Caesarea Philippi separate from the coastal Caesarea in Acts; Antioch in Pisidia separate from Antioch that sends missionaries. Bethany near Jerusalem is not automatically John1's beyond-Jordan baptizing place. Magdala/Dalmanutha and Gergesenes/Gadarenes must not be silently merged as identical source strings or precise archaeological sites.
3. Preserve each Gospel's sequence. Matthew20/Mark10 place blind-person healing on departure from Jericho; Luke18 describes approach. Matthew/Mark say six days before transfiguration, Luke about eight. John2's temple action comes early in that Gospel; Synoptic placement is late. A single itinerary would conceal these differences.
4. Do not combine Matthew1 and Luke3 into an inferred biological family tree. Keep the named lineages attached to their narrators. Do not identify unnamed women, beloved disciple, fleeing young man, hills, houses or tombs without a separately sourced identity claim.
5. Letters have senders, audiences, reported past travel, present circumstances and intentions; these are different relationship types. Romans15's Spain plan, Titus3's wintering plan and2Corinthians13's planned visit are not completed-route records. Hebrews13's Italy greeting, Philippians4's Caesar-household greeting and1Peter5's Babylon greeting do not independently settle composition city.
6. Reuse `AJ03` first mission and `AJ04` Macedonia crossing, with Acts15–21 providing extensions rather than duplicate tracks. In Acts20 Paul walks to Assos while companions sail; in Acts27 the intended Phoenix/Phenice destination is not reached. A place the ship passes is not a landing.
7. `AJ05` must retain Appii Forum/Three Taverns as grouped meetings, not an asserted chronology of two visits. Acts28 ends with two years of Roman residence; do not append Paul's execution to that chapter's event as though narrated there.
8. Preserve visionary identity: Revelation's Babylon, New Jerusalem, heavenly temple, lake of fire and river of life are not coordinates. Literal terrestrial names inside visions do not turn visions into surveyed future routes. No modern religious/ethnic group is labelled the vision's villain.
9. KJV-specific text questions need editorial notes: Mark16:9–20, John7:53–8:11, the different demoniac-place forms, and footnote/JST treatments remain an official-edition and primary-manuscript review task. This file makes no claim to have examined original manuscripts.
10. The existing engine sends absent site coordinates to 0,0 and does not filter individual notes by layer/time. These records belong in cited text/reference material until an appropriate renderer exists; no unlocated chapter episode goes in `world.sites`.
