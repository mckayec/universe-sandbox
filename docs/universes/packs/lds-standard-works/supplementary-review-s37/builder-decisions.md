## Additional exact correction: BMC-164 / Helaman 6

**S37-C2:** the “namesake” reason is incomplete. [Helaman 6:10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/hel/6?lang=eng) identifies both the land Mulek **and Mulek as Zedekiah's son**. BXP37 carries the land-name distinction; BMV-166 currently cites only Helaman 8. For an economical explicit consolidation, append this to BMV-166 and add Helaman 6:10 as a source:

> Helaman 6:10 separately says that the northern land was named after Mulek, whom that verse calls Zedekiah's son.

Keep the existing attribution/uncorroborated-genealogy safeguard and Nephi's Helaman 8 wording. Replace BMC-164 reason with: “The land-name distinction is represented by BXP37; the chapter's attributed Mulek–Zedekiah relation is represented by BMV-166 with its additional Helaman 6:10 source. Neither relation proves a modern continental assignment or independent genealogy.” Do not mark that consolidation complete before the added citation and sentence exist.
