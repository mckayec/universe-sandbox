## Evidence and method

The underlying140-unit official-source full reading is retained in coverage-dc.md/csv. This second record-to-source pass compared every registry definition with that reading and the retained official bodies/headings; high-density52/75/102/124 rosters,107ordination chain,84transmission chain, travel instructions60–61/84/124, OD attachments and source-specific ancient identities were reread directly. Mechanical name occurrence screening helped locate evidence but was not treated as proof of identity or a typed relationship. Each138-person row was inspected; abbreviated, heading-only and source-silent expansions were checked separately. Current editorial headings, footnotes, canonical body, retrospective narration and actual historical evidence remain distinct.

Private reproducible witnesses: /workspace/lds-source-evidence/20261026/dc/manifest.csv, capture-manifest.json, body-validation.json, normalized bodies and original capture windows. dc-source-witnesses.csv is public metadata, not raw scripture. No registry family is left awaiting basic canonical copy-to-source checking. Unresolved co-reference, ancient geography, archival corroboration, full historical biographies, missing unnamed participants and present-day gazetteer coordinates remain honest limits. Those limits require qualified rendering or optional deeper evidence, not invented facts. This audit is not an exhaustive token census of every proper noun outside the defined registries.
