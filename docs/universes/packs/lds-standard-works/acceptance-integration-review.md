## CA03: research disposition versus implementation

The identity and integration files provide explicit reuse, source-specific gates, city/region distinctions, contained settings, intended versus completed journeys, quotation/vision modes and deliberate non-merges. These are usable research decisions even before app serialization. Same-name figures must remain context-scoped where identity is not established. A genealogical chain, office roster or named-settlement list can be a reference-family payload; it does not need a speculative physical pin or invented biological parent edge.

The new dense CSVs require an explicit import contract because their keys and meaning differ: JAR context keys, LHR source/context expressions, OHF family payloads, NTPR typed assertions and NTRX source-context chains. Blank JAR `canonical_reuse` means retain the context record, not infer a duplicate physical location. LHR adjacency is expressly not parenthood; its literal relation expressions must not be promoted into global identities without the documented qualification. These contracts and their internal references can be checked now. Pack-object ID existence, transformed links, filtering and screen behavior can only be verified against a future build and exact SHA.

A shared entity at gate1 does not lower an NT event or a BoM/D&C interpretation to1. `mp-a02` and `mp-a06` explicitly preserve BoM interpretation gate3; `mp-a01` preserves D&C interpretation gate4. This is stronger than treating all Bible heading prose as harmless gate1 copy. Carry these overrides into the master handoff.
