# Old Testament late history — complete chapter reading ledger

2026-10-26. Scope: 1–2 Chronicles, Ezra, Nehemiah and Esther: **98 chapters / 2,617 verses**. All verse bodies in the disclosed KJV transcription were read for atlas relevance, including genealogies and administrative lists. A later complete official verse-body collation covers all 98 chapters; this is still **not** a claim that every heading, footnote or historical question has been verified. No chapter is excluded as irrelevant merely because it is genealogical, legal or repetitive. The CSV records per-unit source, inspected extent, proposed record ID, exclusions and remaining work.

## Evidence and implementation contract

`(C-T)` below means `(C)` for what the opened, read **third-party KJV transcription** says; it is not a new confidence category and does not claim official-edition identity. `O` sources are official Church crosschecks explicitly listed below. Main-text/footnote differences must remain labelled. The fallback was used after official web requests returned HTTP429; it is a provenance limitation, not proof that official sources are permanently inaccessible. All five JSON files were downloaded again at pinned commit `a9aa4e55afbb3e095f57e4b14cd1f22c5ee8d7c9` and matched byte-for-byte. No biblical text is republished here beyond short terms.

All new records: `layer:bible`, `first:1` (existing OT navigator), `parent:earth` where meaningful, `when:omitted`, `lat/lon:omitted`. Chapter references identify the actual source disclosure; first1 is **not** a claim to protection at chapter granularity. This file's chapter records are nonspatial explanatory/reference entries by default. Existing Jerusalem P08 and Babylon AP07 are reused; their modern anchors do not locate a specific ancient action. Other named places require deduplication with the earlier-history inventory, then text-only handling until supported modern anchors are supplied. No ancient journey geometry, palace blueprint, population estimate or divine event is established by a scripture citation alone.

**Text:** each ledger row's summary is an attributed narrative claim. **Official teaching:** only the identified Church main text/footnotes are crosschecked, not a separate doctrine survey. **History/scholarship:** no independent historical verification is claimed by this file. **Geography:** named cities, regions and internal relations are textual; no modern identification is smuggled in through a familiar spelling. No claim here rests on a film, a wiki plot summary or model geography.

## Chapter-level atlas records

Each ID is a usable short reference record with its cited passage and explicit exclusion, not 98 additional map pins. The final column records what remains omitted or unsafe, not permission to ignore material passages. Sources T1–T5 are mapped by book in the source register. `(C-T)` applies to each narrative summary; exclusions are editorial dispositions.
