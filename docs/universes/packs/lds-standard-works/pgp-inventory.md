# Pearl of Great Price — component coverage and textual inventory

2026-10-26; baseline main `cd85ea8dcb36d4d5b54afd5ec05bc03c2c173d79`. Supplements `research.md`, preserving its P/E/J/C IDs. This closes the previous absence of creation/Fall/Noah, Abraham 1/4/5, facsimiles and the remaining Articles of Faith from the **selected atlas inventory**. It does not claim a verse-by-verse audit or a completed independent history of the text.

All new records use navigator `first:5` / `book:5`, `layers:["pgp"]`, and terrestrial `parent:earth` where a parent is meaningful. Dates are absent unless explicitly stated. Every proposed place below is **unlocated in this dataset**, with no `lat/lon`, no invented `rel`, and no geometry approved for Earth sites. `(C)` means confirmed as an attributed statement from an opened source. S keys refer to `sources.md`; PG keys below are local.

## Remaining PGP work

All five component works now have selected core narrative/concept records, with a 19-row checked-unit ledger. Nine new place records, nine event/reference records, two journeys and ten codex records have citations and explicit nonspatial handling. This is 30 new content records, not 30 newly verified modern locations.

The nonlocatable minor regions and rivers are deliberately covered as grouped reference entries rather than fictional map pins. Cross-layer deduplication with the Bible and restoration supplements remains necessary; this is an integration task, not an access blocker. No standalone historical “proof” of the narratives is required to publish accurately attributed scripture summaries, but any scholarly claims added later need their own appropriate evidence. Figure-by-figure Egyptological comparison is uncompleted scholarship, not a license to present captions as established history.
