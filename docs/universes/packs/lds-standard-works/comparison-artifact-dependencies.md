## Dependency boundaries

1. **Current Earth atlas:** the artifacts are **not strictly required** to correct BA01–07 or accept the Earth/reference content on its own merits. Keep unverified comparison claims absent. Existing sourced nonspatial scripture summaries can be built without them. Technical/final audit and owner release approval remain required for the atlas itself.
2. **Internal text-relation schematic:** owner design approval is already recorded; do not ask again. The seven cited relation rows and safeguards in A03 support implementation work independently of the missing originals. Distances, scale, modern coordinates and guessed intermediate relations remain prohibited. A completed schematic still needs its own implementation/content checks; approval of its concept is not release approval.
3. **Paired archaeology/LiDAR comparison:** review of the owner's existing work **does require the identifiable originals and their source context**. Until received, no endorsement, evidence comparison or completeness claim about those originals is possible. This is a separate requested feature/evidence review, not a retroactive prerequisite for the existing Earth pack or permission to replace the originals with generated material.

Once provided, separate textual assertion/chronology, survey observation/footprint, ground verification, archaeological dates/method and attributed proposed association. LiDAR can reveal terrain and possible structures; it does not alone determine ethnicity, religion, named persons or equivalence with a textual city. Acquisition/publication/occupation dates remain distinct. No supported geographical uncertainty is resolved by owner preference alone.
