# Restoration inventory — sourced supplement

Research completed 2026-10-26, against the checkpoint in `research.md` based on main `cd85ea8dcb36d4d5b54afd5ec05bc03c2c173d79`. This supplement adds **13 places, 20 events, 3 journeys and 6 codex entries**, plus contextual updates to existing P12/P14/P15. It covers the requested Restoration gaps as text-ready records, not the entire D&C or an authorization to build. Local IDs use RP/RE/RJ/RC to avoid collisions with parallel inventories. Existing P12 = Fayette/Whitmer home, P14 = Kirtland Temple, P15 = Nauvoo; do not duplicate those places.

**Evidence convention:** `(C)` confirms the attributed statement from an opened source. **Text/belief** records what a scripture or participant reports; **document history** describes a dated document and its editorial context; **historical synthesis** describes a source's historical findings. Neither Church publication nor a modern heritage marker independently proves a supernatural experience. All sources R01–R20 below were opened and the relevant passages read. No dedicated Deep Research tool was used.

**Design:** every record belongs to `dc-history`, navigator `first:4` under the checkpoint scheme. The Joseph Smith—History material can additionally appear in `pgp` only through separately labelled content at navigator 5; do not silently lower its disclosure gate. Dates given as full ISO strings are research metadata; the current engine `when` uses the year only. No year zero, guessed coordinates or precise route geometry is authorized. All quotations in tables are proposed original display copy, not transcriptions of the sources.

## 5. Evidence distinctions, corrections and unresolved issues

1. **Independent migration context:** NPS places Winter Quarters on Omaha land and describes disputes involving the Omaha and federal Indian Affairs officials. Exact short place note: “This camp stood on Omaha land, amid disputes over access to land and resources.” [R13] (C) Use this alongside emigrant hardship; do not describe an empty wilderness or reduce all opposition to religious persecution. Tribal-authored history has not yet been opened, so this is not a substitute for an Omaha perspective.
2. **Winter Quarters month discrepancy:** NPS’s timeline says September 1846; its complex page describes relocation from Cutler’s Park in October. [R11][R13] (C) RE10 therefore uses autumn/year precision. Do not silently choose one month; a fuller settlement-sequence investigation remains needed. Population estimates also vary across those pages; no population count is approved here.
3. **Location versus experience:** the official Priesthood Restoration Site page calls the sugar-bush location *likely*. [R04] (C) Its monuments and reconstructed buildings are commemorative evidence, not exact surveyed proof of the reported angelic event. RP01 and RP02 are separate settings.
4. **Liberty chronology:** the current D&C121 heading explicitly uses two epistles, March 20 and approximately March 22. [R06] (C) The jail placement and transfer date are separately supported by JSP. [R07] (C) A full account of charges, hearings and escape is beyond the short inventory; do not infer conviction or acquittal.
5. **Missouri violence:** the state archive supplies an external account and a primary-document finding aid. [R08][R09] (C) This inventory does not assign a massacre casualty count: figures need comparison of named victims and later deaths before use. The archival overview’s description of Book of Mormon ancestry is also not adopted as the Church’s current doctrine.
6. **No collapsed timeline:** D&C137’s 1836 report, D&C138’s 1918 report and leadership acceptance, and the separate 1978 experience/letter/conference dates are different record types. [R15][R16][R19] (C) The official PGP introduction, now cited by coverage-pgp.md QG16, records their1976 addition and1979 transfer to D&C; independent documentary collation remains; RE16 is **not** labelled 1918 canonization by general conference.
7. **No full historical consensus claim:** the 20 sources include Church scripture, Church history, a documentary edition, NPS and Missouri State Archives. JSP and official Church pages are not independent of the Church. External history is strongest here for migration and Missouri records, not for the religious experiences or every doctrinal-policy transition.
8. **Coverage boundary:** the requested gaps now have usable entries. Still excluded: full section-by-section D&C inventory; the entire Ohio/Missouri/Nauvoo chronology; plural-marriage individual experiences; Black members’ first-person histories; broader Indigenous perspectives; original-source transcription audit; most coordinates and exact route geometry. No status change or canon approval follows from this supplement.
