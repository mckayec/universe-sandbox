### S14-C1 — SD0849 / DR14 / DCM-dr14-gause-williams

The sentence “The 1832 instruction and its 1833 replacement are different documents” invents a document distinction. The [section 81 heading](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/81?lang=eng) describes transfer of the call and Williams's later ordination, while the section retains its March 1832 date. Replace the whole description with:

> Doctrine and Covenants — The heading of section 81 says the March 15, 1832 revelation originally called Jesse Gause as a counselor to Joseph Smith. The call was later transferred to Frederick G. Williams, who was ordained to that office on March 18, 1833. The current text names Williams. These are distinct people and a succession in office, not aliases or evidence here of a separate 1833 revelation.

Acceptance: remove “different documents”; keep original recipient, transferred appointment and dates distinct. Keep `4/dc`, current source and no geometry. This corrects a builder expansion of the accepted shorthand “1832 instruction vs 1833 replacement,” not a newly required manuscript study.

### S14-C2 — SD0813 / DJ03 / DCM-dj03-salem-journey

“The heading names four travelers” omits the requested named participants even though the source is accessible. The [section 111 heading](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/111?lang=eng) identifies them. Replace the description with:

It reports that they returned to Kirtland when no money was forthcoming. This is historical context in the editorial heading, distinct from the revelation's own text.

Acceptance: retain all four distinct travelers, round-trip towns, heading attribution and no inferred road, treasure location or recovered money. No new person IDs are required for this textual repair.
