# Pearl of Great Price — complete unit screening and atlas additions

2026-10-26. Comprehensive scope was chosen by the owner after the first draft PR. This pass reads the complete numbered text of all 16 chapters and all three published facsimile explanations from the official Church pages, including continuation paragraphs. It is an atlas-content screening, not independent authentication of ancient events, a manuscript collation, or an Egyptological translation. The 19 unit rows in `coverage-pgp.csv` expose review depth and remaining integration work. Articles of Faith are further broken out below. Front matter and the appended Cowdery account are separately identified.

`(C)` means confirmed as an attributed textual statement. Every row below is `(C)` for that limited purpose. No modern coordinates, astronomical catalog matches, absolute ancient dates, or route geometry are added. Existing P/PP/PE/PJ/PC keys refer to `research.md` and `pgp-inventory.md`; QG keys are additional research records in this file. They are not ready-to-serialize engine objects. All PGP source claims use `first:5`, `layers:["pgp"]`; no layer-specific claim belongs in the ungated base notes of a shared site.

## Audit corrections and remaining boundaries

1. Add Moses6:42's sea-east movement to the Enoch itinerary reference. Existing PJ01 is Enos's journey, not Enoch's; do not merge travelers.
2. Distinguish the two Enochs, two Lamechs, Cainan person/land, Canaan people/land, Mahijah person/Mahujah place, and Abraham's Jershon/Book of Mormon Jershon. Equal strings do not license equal identities.
3. PC01's earlier phrase “delivered by an angel” requires Abraham1:15–16 in addition to Facsimile1 captions; the caption alone labels figures. Add that cross-source citation before reuse.
4. QG14 chronology retains JS—H1:62's December arrival context and the different translation episodes;1829-04-07 is not asserted to be the beginning of all translation. The subsequent Fayette move belongs to other sources, not this chapter.
5. This completes a textual atlas screening of PGP's19 units, not the whole LDS handoff.
