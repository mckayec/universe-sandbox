# Old Testament wisdom and prophets — passage coverage

2026-10-26; scope Job–Song of Solomon and Daniel–Malachi, 18 books, 322 chapters; Isaiah–Ezekiel are tracked separately in coverage-ot-major-prophets.csv. **Checkpoint: 322/322 complete chapter verse bodies inspected, all 322 supported by primary chapter text (38 directly read, 284 fully collated after transcription reading); 0 pending.** A retrieved file is not a reviewed chapter. This completes the bounded textual, apparatus and material-integration pass for this shard; it is not a whole-canon approval or independent verification of narrated history.

## Evidence and coverage method

The companion [chapter ledger](coverage-ot-wisdom-prophets.csv) contains one explicit row per chapter, with no unrepresented chapter. `inspected` means all verses in the stated source were read and given an atlas disposition, not that historical truth or official edition equivalence was established. `records` points to chapter-level source cards and applicable stable material references below; these are usable extraction records, not ready-to-serialize engine sites.

Official Church KJV web pages were used where available. Direct shell retrieval of a Church page returned proxy HTTP403, while web opens succeeded; this is a transport limitation, not an inaccessible canon claim. To sustain the full-text pass, the remaining chapters use the explicitly identified third-party [aruljohn/Bible-kjv transcription](https://github.com/aruljohn/Bible-kjv). All 22 files were fetched successfully and chapter/verse numbering checked. Their source snapshots are hashed below. No assertion of equality with the Church's current edition, editorial headings, footnotes, JST notes or Psalm superscriptions follows from this transcription. The complete150-Psalm body was collated verse by verse to retrieved official pages after reading the transcription;143 chapters match after punctuation/case normalization. Nine word/spelling differences across seven chapters were individually inspected:27:4 enquire/inquire;45:12 intreat/entreat;50:17 casteth/castest;71:18 greyheaded/grayheaded;78:8,37 stedfast/steadfast and78:34 enquired/inquired;119:58 intreated/entreated;148:8 vapours/vapour. The Church wording governs. No missing verse remains. All150 superscription regions and editorial headings were inspected separately; the bounded atlas-relevant inline-note audit is complete below; linked reference chains are not exhaustively expanded. The remaining134 non-Psalm chapter bodies were also completely collated:120 match normalized words, while16 differing verses across14 chapters were individually inspected. Their small spelling/word variants include Job8:8,10:6,11:15,16:5–6,30:6; Proverbs18:23,19:6,20:25,29; Ecclesiastes7:10,8:17; Daniel1:20,5:5,6:26; Amos2:2. Notably the official Amos2:2 spelling is **Kerioth**, not transcription Kirioth. Thus all322 chapter-body summaries now have primary textual support; no claim of entire-edition/footnote equality is made. `(C)` means the cited primary text supports the attributed textual claim, never independent proof of the event or miracle.

Types: N narrative; J journey/direction; E event; O oracle; V vision/promise; P poetry; C explanatory concept. Each record's first reading gate is OT (`first:1`), layer Bible, evidence text unless explicitly labelled otherwise. All numeric dates, coordinates and route geometry are withheld. Regnal and relative dates stay as text. Shared places must resolve through the master alias registry; do not create a new Jerusalem, Babylon, Bethlehem, Horeb or Mount of Olives for each mention. An oracle or poetic comparison is not a physical journey. Prophetic threats are attributed text, not this atlas endorsing violence against modern peoples.

All reviewed chapters have substantive conceptual or narrative content; no whole chapter was dismissed as irrelevant simply because it lacks a new map pin. Exclusions concern mapping or unsupported inference, while conceptual material remains indexed. Full records below preserve people/relationships, places, event sequence, chronology and explanatory themes where present.

### Chapter-to-material references and nonnarrative exclusions

The companion CSV `records` field now lists the chapter's stable `WP-book-nnn` record plus applicable WP material IDs and existing P/AP references. A chapter record is itself a usable **nonspatial source card**: its type distinguishes narrative, oracle, vision, poetry and concept; its verses, summary and exclusion define the content. Chapters without a separately named card are not dropped or marked unreviewed. Wisdom instructions, prayers and anonymous illustrative scenes remain conceptual source cards; their generic houses, roads, pits, kingdoms and speakers are explicitly excluded from new identifiable map entities when the text supplies no independent identity. Repeated references attach to existing entities, not duplicate site cards. Named poetic comparisons create labelled reference edges, not itineraries.

A safe implementation can consume this research without new facts: display source cards and unresolved names in a searchable textual index; represent journey status as completed/intended/interrupted/visionary/collective; store uncertain timing as text; render only already sourced neutral anchors on Earth. Numeric coordinates, polygons or dates are optional and must remain absent where unsupported. The absence of an engine feature is an implementation task, not unfinished textual research. Additional archaeological identifications or proposed geography models would be separately attributed optional enrichment, not prerequisites to retaining honest unknowns.

## Integration and evidence issues

- **Printed Bible versus inspiration qualifier:** all eight Song of Solomon chapters are included in coverage. The official [Song1 note](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/song/1?lang=eng) reports the JST manuscript characterizes the Songs as not inspired writings. Display as biblical poetry with that explicitly attributed LDS study-note context; do not silently drop the book or present it as an unqualified LDS doctrinal authority. (C for the study note.)
- Jonah reaches Nineveh, not Tarshish; Joppa departure and intended Tarshish destination must not create a completed voyage. The release beach is unnamed.
- Hosea8:13/9:3 return-to-Egypt language and11:5 not-return-to-Egypt remain visible without harmonization. Hosea3 does not name its woman; a Gomer identity is not silently added.
- Amos7 records Amaziah's allegation about Amos as speech by Amaziah. A departure command to Judah is not proof Amos made that trip.
- Zechariah5–6 separates visionary transport from reported captives returning from Babylon. Joshua the priest is distinct from Joshua son of Nun.
- Zechariah14 supplies an OT Mount of Olives reference; official chapter cross-check completed; update existing P20 earliest work gate from NT2 to OT1, retaining later layer-specific claims separately. Future split-mountain geography must not modify the present Earth mesh.
- Later Christian/LDS interpretations of messianic passages, Elijah, temple and gathering need separately attributed sources/cross-links. Their presence in this OT extraction does not assert one interpretation as an independent historical finding.
- No archaeological chronologies, competing modern identifications, authorship consensus or full scholarly editions have been verified in this checkpoint. These remain separate work, not purported full-source access.
