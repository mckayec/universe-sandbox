# Old Testament coverage: Genesis–2 Kings

2026-10-26. Supplement to the LDS standard-works research. Scope: 12 books, 338 chapters; 1 Chronicles–Esther are in `coverage-ot-late-history.md`. **Work in progress, not research-ready.** The companion CSV ledger distinguishes inspected chapter text from pending chapters. A page fetch alone is not a completed research pass.

## Evidence and rendering policy

Chapter-specific links refer to the Church's English KJV. `(C)` confirms support for an attributed textual claim, not historical verification. Editorial chapter headings and footnotes are distinguished from verses. All records below use `layer:bible`, `first:1`; exact book/chapter ranges remain the finer provenance. Numeric chronology and Earth geometry are withheld unless independently supported. Names recurring in other traditions do not automatically identify the same person or place. Every geographical sequence is schematic; no surveyed road is asserted.

The scope is atlas relevance: material places, journeys, events, people/relationships and explanatory concepts. Legal or genealogical passages can contribute concepts/relationships without adding a separate pin. Explicit exclusion means no **new spatial record** is warranted by the screened material, not that the chapter has no religious importance. Existing core IDs and AP/AE/AJ/AC records must be reused during integration.
