## Denominators and what was actually checked

Three different quantities must remain separate:

1. **Corpus intake: 17/17 named files structurally screened**, with the exact snapshot below. Every Markdown table data row was included in the structural count; headers and separator rows were excluded. Rows include chapter summaries, repeated crosswalks, sources, audit findings and records. They are **not unique entities, atomic factual claims, or a human source-verification numerator**. The lexical uncertainty screen is a reproducible retrieval aid, not a semantic classification: `unknown|unlocated|uncertain|unresolved|propos|candidate|disput|no (modern|exact|precise)|not (a|an) (modern|verified)|vision|prophe`, case-insensitive. Prose and all primary registries were also considered in the family audit below.
2. **Explicit low-confidence content labels: zero literal `(L)` content assertions found** in these files. Occurrences explaining the confidence notation or saying that no such assertions are offered are excluded. This does not mean all geographical identities are certain. Unlocated, visionary and proposed-model records are treated as uncertainty families below even where their correctly attributed textual claim is marked C.
3. **Source re-verification is not the same as intake.** The 73 core/PGP records are being individually checked in `core-pgp-audit.md`; that file's final per-record outcome governs, not this sentence. The wisdom/minor-prophet continuation has 51 material additions, five existing-place updates, 322 body dispositions, and 28 inline-apparatus audit families with actual source reading/collation documented in its own file. The other shards disclose their own source reading and integration. This audit reviewed the risk rules and material geographical/journey registries across those scopes, but does **not** claim a second independent reading of every named person or every compound sentence in all 1,587 units. Its fresh targeted opens were Ether9:1–28, Acts28:1–31 and Isaiah7:1–25 (plus displayed apparatus), supporting the specific findings below. Those three opens add no independent historical corroboration.

**Checklist status:** whole-corpus risk-family screening is supplied here; the strict “open a source for every prominent claim and report checked/total” acceptance gate cannot honestly be declared complete from a structural row count or inherited C labels. A completed ledger from each owning shard can close that gate without inventing atomic claim totals. Use stable record/family IDs as the denominator and keep compound-family sources explicit. Any unchecked sentence must remain attributed/source-linked or withheld, never silently upgraded to independently corroborated history.

## Independent corroboration and remaining acceptance boundary

The ten-case matrix remains the external-source audit, not ten independent confirmations. UNESCO supports modern heritage properties and institutional descriptions; NPS and Missouri State Archives support selected modern historical claims; a Church historical essay/JSP editorial account is not independent of the Church. Two scriptural accounts or a scripture plus a Church teaching page are not two independent archaeological witnesses. Nebes supplies primary epigraphic evidence, but the inference to Nahom is separate. A proponent's article establishes the proponent's position, not the position's truth. No unsupported claim of scholarly consensus is approved.

**True research acceptance work at this snapshot:** finish the explicit prominent-record/source-support accounting required by WC04; resolve WC01–03 or withhold affected copy; reconcile current apparatus/source-access limits in the owning ledgers rather than carrying historical checkpoint language forward. Remaining full critical-book reading, exact BoM model geometry, every biblical archaeological identification, exact unknown ancient dates, and authentication of reported religious experiences are not automatically prerequisites for a correctly attributed, nonspatial atlas. They become blockers only if the proposed display asserts those identifications or historical conclusions.

**Genuine access/unknown boundaries:** FIRM pages503 and Maxwell403 are recorded source-access failures, not permission denials. Some primary map editions and the complete Ritner critical edition have not been read; “unread” is not “proved inaccessible.” Precise Peter/James/John visitation date, Moroni's final travel route, most modern narrative-place identities, and a single reconciled account of textual differences are not supplied by the inspected evidence. The safe copy preserves those unknowns. No bypass or unsupported substitute is authorized.

**Later implementation/release gates:** nonspatial serialization, source-specific gates, safe ungated-codex notice, cross-link validation in built data, rendering, exact-SHA checks, builder/content sign-off and owner publication/release decisions remain necessary at their respective stages. They should not be misreported as unread scripture. This document neither merges/deploys nor changes any task status. Parent must record the resulting audit commit SHA after concurrent edits settle; working-file hashes above identify this audit's intake snapshot only.
