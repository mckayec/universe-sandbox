# LDS atlas: major prophets chapter coverage

Research checkpoint, 2026-10-26. **171/171 chapters substantively inspected (4083 verses); 0 pending.** Chapter-body screening and complete official verse-body collation are complete; material heading/footnote interpretation and final atlas serialization remain. Every chapter is explicitly listed with its own disposition.

## Source and rendering contract

Inspected means every verse body in the pinned third-party public-domain KJV transcription was read and the chapter received an atlas disposition; it does not mean the official Church edition, headings, footnotes or JST were exhaustively compared. `(C-T)` denotes C-confidence for the attributed transcription text only, not historical truth or a new confidence category. Official chapter links in the ledger are navigation targets, not claims those pages were read. Nine selected official full-chapter readings are recorded separately below. A later complete official verse-body collation covers all 171 chapters, with manual lexical-difference review and durable captured witnesses in the final section. Headings, footnotes and JST are not exhaustively collated. The downloaded master copies were verified byte-identical to the pinned commit. No Deep Research capability is claimed.

Prophecy, vision, poetic personification and retrospective narration must retain distinct record types. No automatic contemporary fulfillment, fabricated coordinates or absolute dates. Default `layer:bible`, `first:1`; unresolved places and concepts belong in the reference library rather than unsafe map fallbacks. No numeric `when`, latitude or longitude is supplied here. Existing Jerusalem/Babylon records should be cross-linked rather than duplicated. Each MP identifier is a chapter-level record family, not a claim every name has an independently sourced map entity.

## Official Church cross-checks actually read

Verse4 addresses Babylon’s king; the heading and footnotes also teach Lucifer’s rebellion. All unsourced coordinates and absolute `when` values remain absent. Scripture supports an attributed narrative, oracle or vision, not independent historical verification. Source links below identify the cited passages; official collation status remains governed by the chapter ledger and official-check register, rather than by the presence of an official URL.

Other stable slugs are proposed cross-shard dedup keys: match the early-history/poetry registry by identity and passage before creating a new record.
