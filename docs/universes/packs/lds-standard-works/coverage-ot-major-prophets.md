# LDS atlas: major prophets chapter coverage

Research checkpoint, 2026-10-26. **171/171 chapters substantively inspected (4083 verses); 0 pending.** Chapter-body screening is complete; official-edition comparison and atlas integration are not complete. Every chapter is explicitly listed with its own disposition.

## Source and rendering contract

Inspected means every verse body in the pinned third-party public-domain KJV transcription was read and the chapter received an atlas disposition; it does not mean the official Church edition, headings, footnotes or JST were exhaustively compared. `(C-T)` denotes C-confidence for the attributed transcription text only, not historical truth or a new confidence category. Official chapter links in the ledger are navigation targets, not claims those pages were read. Nine official full-chapter checks are recorded separately below; all other official edition comparisons remain pending. The downloaded master copies were verified byte-identical to the pinned commit. No Deep Research capability is claimed.

Prophecy, vision, poetic personification and retrospective narration must retain distinct record types. No automatic contemporary fulfillment, fabricated coordinates or absolute dates. Default `layer:bible`, `first:1`; unresolved places and concepts belong in the reference library rather than unsafe map fallbacks. No numeric `when`, latitude or longitude is supplied here. Existing Jerusalem/Babylon records should be cross-linked rather than duplicated. Each MP identifier is a chapter-level record family, not a claim every name has an independently sourced map entity.

## Official Church cross-checks actually read

O1: [Isaiah14](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/14?lang=eng), entire32-verse body and relevant footnotes read. Verse4 addresses Babylon’s king; the heading and footnotes also teach Lucifer’s rebellion. Footnotes apply verses12–15 to either ruler or Lucifer and explain the north-mountain image through Babylonian belief. The atlas must preserve textual address and attributed Church interpretation together. Philistia is the editorial explanation of Palestina, not the modern political territory.

O2: [Isaiah20](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/20?lang=eng), entire6-verse body and footnotes read. The Ashdod/Sargon/Tartan context is explicit. The Church’s editorial date is approximately711BCE; the clothing gloss describes lack of an upper garment. These are labeled editorial explanations rather than silently inserted into the main verse.

O3: [Isaiah53](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/53?lang=eng), entire12-verse body and heading read. The Church interprets the suffering figure as Messiah and directs comparison with Mosiah14; this supports an attributed doctrinal cross-link, not independent archaeological evidence for a grave.

These checks do not establish official-edition equivalence for the remainder. Cross-canon textual differences, particularly Isaiah passages reused in the Book of Mormon, still require separate comparison rather than copy-equivalence assumptions.

O4: [Jeremiah27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/27?lang=eng), entire22-verse body read. Main verse1 retains Jehoiakim while verses3/12 name Zedekiah. The available footnote links do not erase that tension; a timeline must disclose it.

O5: [Jeremiah43](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jer/43?lang=eng), entire13-verse body read. Jeremiah and Baruch accompany the remnant to Tahpanhes; the later Babylonian conquest is predicted, not narrated as completed. Beth-shemesh is explicitly in Egypt.

O6: [Lamentations5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/lam/5?lang=eng), entire22-verse body read. The official heading attributes the prayer to Jeremiah; the verse body itself speaks collectively and does not name its speaker. Preserve attributed traditional authorship separately from internal narrative evidence.

O7: [Ezekiel37](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/37?lang=eng), entire28-verse body and relevant heading/footnotes read. The Church heading teaches resurrection, Bible/Book of Mormon unity and David as Messiah; verse22 explicitly explains national reunion. Both must remain visible. Footnotes link D&C27:5, 1Nephi13:41 and 2Nephi3:12; those target passages are integration links, not additionally read by this check.

O8: [Ezekiel40](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/40?lang=eng), entire49-verse body and selected footnotes read. The official heading and verse2 explicitly call this a vision. The Church’s approximate long-cubit conversion is53cm and reed3.2m; these can support an explicitly approximate schematic, never a surveyed building footprint.

O9: [Ezekiel47](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ezek/47?lang=eng), entire23-verse body and heading read. The heading identifies the healed sea as the Dead Sea; the river remains within Ezekiel’s temple vision. Resident foreigners’ inheritance is explicit in verses22–23 and must not disappear from an allotment diagram.
