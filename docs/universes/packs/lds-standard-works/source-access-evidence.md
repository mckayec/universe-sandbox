# Source-access evidence and reading provenance

2026-10-26. This document addresses the independent QA finding that a populated ledger or artifact hash cannot independently establish that source bodies were read. **Capture, comparison, reading, extraction and corroboration are separate claims.** No manifest can prove the researcher's mental act of reading; it can preserve the actual witness that was available and make the resulting research auditable.

## Durable evidence

Raw source captures are preserved outside the public repository at `/workspace/lds-source-evidence/20261026/`. Public `*-source-witnesses.csv` files contain metadata only: unit, witness, actual retrieval date, URL, completeness limits and SHA256 fingerprints. Content-addressed capture filenames identify exact saved responses; later verification is a new capture, not a retroactive replacement of evidence. Git commit history makes each published metadata snapshot immutable by reference to its commit SHA. The private raw archive is retained for parent/QA inspection; it is not claimed to be a public web archive or a perpetual guarantee of upstream availability.

A pinned upstream byte-identical transcription snapshot, where verified, supports reproducibility; it does not turn a third-party transcription into the Church edition. ## What the checks mean

A **capture hash** fingerprints the exact saved tool response, including wrapper/line metadata. A **normalized-body hash** fingerprints the identified body after documented removal of line/link markup; differing normalization methods are specified by the shard exporter and must not be assumed interchangeable. A **full-body comparison** can establish that an already-read transcription's words match the retrieved official text after normalization, with each substantive difference inspected. It does not establish equality of edition apparatus, punctuation, manuscript tradition or history.

`validate-coverage.py` checks unit enumeration and ledger structure. `full-body-validation.md` hashes research artifacts at a checkpoint. Neither is a source-access log, an independent reading certificate or a historical audit. Source-manifest metadata and the actual private capture bytes must be used together for provenance verification. Run `python validate-source-witnesses.py --archive /path/to/20261026` from this folder to verify capture hashes; the private archive must be available. A missing archive or missing shard manifest fails this check rather than silently passing. Normalized-body hashes retain each exporter’s separate normalization contract.
