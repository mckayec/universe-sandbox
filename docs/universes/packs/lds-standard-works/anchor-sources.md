## Gazetteer points

Opened the actual ToposText place records and read the identity, coordinate and confidence fields for Antioch (362362PAnt), Seleucia Pieria (361359USel), Salamis (352339PSal), Paphos (348324PPap), Iconium (379325PIko), Lystra (376323ULys), Attalia (369307PAtt), Troas (398262UAnt), Syracuse (371153PSyr), Rhegium (381156PRhe), Puteoli (408141PDik) and Patmos (373265IPat). Eleven report High confidence; Lystra reports Low and says possibly Hatunsaray. These are scholarly gazetteer records, not excavator field reports. Direct reading verifies what their records report, not independent historicity or meter-level accuracy.

The eleven high-confidence fields supply proposed replacements. Patmos is an island record associated with Kastelli: label that representative choice. Antioch's page gives ancient Syria/modern Antakya but its country field says Syria; do not copy this as a modern country designation. Paphos is explicitly Kato Paphos; the separate official Cyprus tourism source supports the Nea/Palae distinction. Linked Pleiades/DARE/Wikidata records were not automatically counted as independent confirmation. ToposText's appended travel narratives were not adopted as precise biblical chronology.

This is not fourteen GNIS/NRHP source checks: those institutional labels in an infobox do not establish that its coordinate was obtained from the named agency. For Independence, Nauvoo, Palmyra and Rome, this bounded pass adds no new direct primary numeric record. The eleven adopted NT replacements do not rely on verification of the superseded Wikipedia coordinates.
