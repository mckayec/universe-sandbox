# Doctrine and Covenants — section-level atlas coverage

Research date: 2026-10-26. Scope is **all 138 sections and both Official Declarations**, not an overview release. This is a live coverage checkpoint; row presence is not completion. The companion `coverage-dc.csv` gives one canonical unit per row and explicit review depth. The earlier `restoration-inventory.md` remains the register for RP/RE/RJ/RC references.

Evidence convention: `(C)` confirms what the cited current official edition states, **not** independent proof of revelation, miracle, alleged motive, prophecy fulfillment or ancient geography. Editorial headings are an official documentary interpretation and are labelled separately from verse claims. All entries are in `dc-history`, proposed work gate `first:4`; claims reused in other layers require their own labels/gates. Dates below usually date a document or reported receipt; they do not date every event recounted inside it. No coordinates or route geometry are approved here.

The scripture pages were opened through the web tool. Direct Python URL retrieval failed with proxy403; initial web429 throttling and timeouts were later resolved. Retrieval alone is not inspection: `heading-only` and `text-partial` rows remain unfinished. No external historical manuscript collation is claimed by this coverage pass.

**Current depth:** 140 inspected;140 total units. These are review units, not140 new map sites.
