# Book of Mormon — comprehensive-scope chapter audit

2026-10-26. Continuation of `bom-inventory.md` and `geography-evidence.md`; baseline documentation PR27. This file expands the research toward the owner's comprehensive standard-works scope. It is **not a declaration of comprehensive completion**. The CSV companion has one row for each of the **239 chapters in all15 books**, so unfinished material cannot disappear into a book-level count.

## Evidence and record contract

`text-partial` means official chapter body available and selected atlas claims assessed, but an all-verse material-entity omission pass remains. `inspected` is reserved for a completed all-verse read with explicit disposition; retrieval alone never earns it. `pending` is work not yet substantively assessed. Earlier selected records are retained, not silently elevated to whole-chapter approval. Each BMC identifier is a chapter-assessment record, **not an additional engine site/event**; do not add this count to the earlier264-record inventory as if all were unique objects.

Every positive claim below is an attributed scriptural narrative or teaching, confidence(C) only for what the text reports. No claim here establishes archaeological occurrence, present-day ethnic ancestry, precise modern location or calibrated historical year. All have `layer:bom`, proposed whole-work navigator `first:3`; exact chapter references, not that coarse gate, govern future chapter-level reading controls. Numeric `when` and geographic coordinates are absent.

Source URLs are official current Church scripture chapter pages, opened through the web tool. Direct Python retrieval was blocked by a proxy403; initial parallel web requests hit429. Paced single-chapter web requests subsequently succeeded. Some multi-open responses returned navigation without enough body; those were not treated as inspected. Raw working captures are outside the repository in `/tmp/bom-cache*.json`; the citations remain independently retrievable URLs, not dependencies on those temporary files.

The official [2013 printed Book of Mormon](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/bc/content/shared/content/english/pdf/language-materials/34406_eng.pdf) was located and its frontmatter inspected as a fallback, **not read in full**. Current webpage headings and older printed headings need not be identical. Chapter summaries are study aids and are not substituted for the numbered verses. No Deep Research capability is claimed.

### People and relationships: minimum safe disambiguation

- **Lehi/Sariah household:** Laman,Lemuel,Sam,Nephi are named early; Jacob and Joseph are born in the wilderness; unnamed sisters accompany Nephi's withdrawal. Do not invent their names or spouses. [1Nephi2:5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/2?lang=eng),[18:7](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/18?lang=eng),[2Nephi5:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/2-ne/5?lang=eng) (C).
- **Record lineage:** Jacob → son Enos → son Jarom → son Omni → son Amaron → brother Chemish → son Abinadom → son Amaleki; childless Amaleki transfers plates to Benjamin. This is a custody chain with mixed relationship types,not a uniform father-to-son pedigree. [Jacob7:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/7?lang=eng),[Jarom1:1,15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jarom/1?lang=eng),[Omni1:1–12,25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng) (C).
- **Mosiah identities:** Omni's Mosiah is Benjamin's father; Benjamin's son Mosiah is the later king. **Ammon identities:** Mosiah7's search leader descends from Zarahemla; Alma17's missionary is a son of the later Mosiah. [Omni1:23](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng),[Mosiah1:2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/1?lang=eng),[7:3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/7?lang=eng),[27:34](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/27?lang=eng) (C).
- **Zeniff → Noah → Limhi** are father/son generations. **Elder Alma → younger Alma** are father/son; elder Alma's association with Noah's priesthood does not make the younger Alma Noah's priest. [Mosiah7:9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/7?lang=eng),[27:8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/mosiah/27?lang=eng) (C).
- **Lamoni's family and allies:** Lamoni's father and wife are unnamed in Alma19–22; Antiomno is king of Middoni and Lamoni's friend. Abish is a named woman acting from prior faith; do not conflate her with Lamoni's wife. [Alma19:2–17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/19?lang=eng),[20:4–8](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/20?lang=eng) (C).
- **Zoram identities:** Laban's servant in1Nephi4; chief captain/father of Lehi and Aha inAlma16. The shared name alone is not evidence of one lifespan or a documented pedigree. [1Nephi4:35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/1-ne/4?lang=eng),[Alma16:5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/16?lang=eng) (C).
