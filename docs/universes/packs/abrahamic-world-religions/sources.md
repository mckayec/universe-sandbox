The gazetteer companion adds eight directly opened UNESCO component-coordinate pages; the source URLs and retrieval date accompany every CSV row. Existing Jerusalem and Babylon anchors stay in JU21–22 rather than being counted twice.

Source keys are scoped to their file. A source repeated in two dossiers is not independent corroboration. Primary institutional material establishes institutional self-description; a doctrinal claim is attributed rather than converted to an independently observed event. Population reference year and classification accompany any number. Unknown identities, coordinates and disputed causation remain explicit.

Raw copyrighted source captures are not published. Each dossier records its actual access depth and unsuccessful routes. A search excerpt is not a complete chapter reading. Sources with inadequate body access can remain research leads but do not license stronger display claims. No Deep Research capability is claimed.
