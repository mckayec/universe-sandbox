# Christianity record-to-source audit

Audited 26 October 2026 by a second agent who did not author the Christianity dossier. Result: **73 declared records checked against actual passages on all 33 cited source pages**, with one unsupported period clause removed. This is a bounded source-support verdict for [christianity-research.md](christianity-research.md), not certification of all Christian history or independent validation of institutional religious claims.

Likewise, 27 graph rows are not 27 mutually exclusive denominations: evangelical/Pentecostal affiliations overlap, an association such as OAIC is not a denomination, and movement hubs differ from successor churches.

They are not included in the 73. This audit does not claim atomization of every prose sentence or every implicit semantic edge. It does include the dates, people, places, proposed copy and typed relations of every declared row and all four named network summaries. All rows were marked C; there were no L-marked rows to silently omit.

## Method and actual checks

Opened the exact registered CH01–CH33 URLs through the web tool and read the passages needed by the records. For long navigation-heavy pages, inspected the body around the exact anchor text: CH24's Columbus convention and name-resolution paragraphs, CH28's 1844 vote and 1847 presidency paragraphs, CH29's Amboy appointment sentence, and CH33's Brest/reception paragraphs. The CSV records full source URLs, human-readable passage locators, record-specific findings, retained date precision and qualifications. These are actual passage checks, not a source-tag existence test or a claim that every linked manuscript/book was read.

Prominent checks included:

- CH03/04: Eastern and Oriental Orthodox are different families; council reception and long separation cannot become a single universal foundation event.
- CH05: the 1054 sentences targeted persons; the 7 December 1965 action did not resolve all communion differences. Rome and Istanbul are ceremony contexts.
- CH06/07: Eastern rites and disciplines within Catholic communion are not interchangeable with Eastern Orthodoxy.
- CH08/10/11: Wittenberg1517, Zurich21January1525 and London1612 claims match the stated institutional passages. The Luther door-nailing scene is not imported.
- CH15/20: 31December1831 and 1January1832 remain two chronology entries within one merger history; Lexington comes from the companion narrative.
- CH22: 20May1863 gathering and 21May constitution/commemorated organization remain distinct. CH24 explicitly supports 26July1931 name adoption in Columbus.
- CH28–31: the Latter-day Saint movement is not identical to its Utah successor; the Strang account's institutional viewpoint is disclosed. Amboy6April1860 and the2001Community of Christ name are supported by different sources. Bickerton/Green Oak1862 is catalogue-supported at year precision.
- CH32/33: Utrecht24September1889 is a communion/conference act, not every Old Catholic church's founding. Brest1595–96 and later contested reception remain an interval and process.

## Limits and reproducibility

The earlier Community of Christ direct-page failures remain disclosed in the dossier. This audit did not repeat those failed paths: CH29 and CH30 were accessible and support the narrower retained historical statements. It does not independently recover the unavailable institution's full doctrinal history. The failed guessed Disciples URL similarly is not a blocker for the accessible CH15/20 evidence.

Institutional sources establish what institutions teach or recount, not truth of resurrection, miraculous succession, apostolic founding or restoration claims. WCC's old membership/officeholder data were not adopted as current facts. Source narratives were not flattened into exact ancient dates, contemporary territorial rights or a universal descent tree. Gazetteer coordinates remain a separate heritage-reference review; this audit checks dossier place associations, not surveyed geometry.

No source bodies or wholesale copyrighted captures are included. Passage locators and retrieval date are durable audit notes; they are not immutable source snapshots. The dossier SHA-256 below identifies the reviewed artifact, not proof of source reading:
