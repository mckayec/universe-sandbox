Source keys `JU01–JU24` are local to the source register below. `(C)` means the cited source was opened and supports the attributed statement; it does not authenticate a miracle, make an institution's self-description universal, or establish independent historical corroboration.

## 1. Meta

- Parent universe: `abrahamic-world-religions`; shard: `judaism`.
- Suggested visitor introduction: “Explore Jewish texts, communities and movements across different periods. Connections describe traditions, historical developments and institutions; they do not rank communities or reduce Jewish identity to a single family tree.” Editorial framing, not a sourced historical assertion.
- TrackA; use a historical network beside the Earth map. `first:1` below is a **local Judaism-overlay gate**, to be mapped by the parent to the pack's navigation. It is not a claim that every development appears in Genesis.
- Primary texts inspected for this shard: Genesis12:1–10; Exodus19:1–8; Josephus, *Antiquities*18.1.1–6. Later institutional histories and academic summaries are identified separately. This is not a new full-canon reading pass.
- Theme suggestion: parchment and indigo, with plain text labels. Do not use a single person's portrait or one ritual object as a universal representation of all Jews. Source prose is paraphrased; no third-party artwork is supplied.

## 2. Structure and interpretation

Keep **scriptural ancestry**, **historical development**, **religious movement**, **cultural community**, **institution**, and **modern state** as different node types. “Abrahamic” is a comparative grouping, not an ancient organization with an administrative succession list. A Jewish movement node does not represent all people with Jewish ancestry or identity. Contemporary Jewish life includes denominational, cross-denominational and unaffiliated expressions. [JU16] (C)

The origins layer presents the Torah's covenant stories as text/tradition, alongside historically situated Israelite and Judean communities. The Second Temple period and later rabbinic developments are broad contexts, not exact birthdays for Judaism. The northern kingdom of Israel and southern Judah are political entities; neither is interchangeable with today's State of Israel. [JU01–JU04] (C)

No dataset field should turn an influence or shared textual inheritance into a schism. In particular: no automatic Pharisees→all rabbis, Sadducees→Karaites, Reform→all other modern movements, or Hasidism→all Orthodox Jews. This is an integration restriction based on the limited and qualified evidence below, not a claim that no historical connection exists.

## 7. Open questions and excluded assertions

1. This shard does not establish an exact historical founder/date for Judaism. Patriarchal narrative, ancient communal formation, Second Temple developments and rabbinic institutions answer different questions.
2. No individual site is asserted for Sinai, a patriarchal campsite or an unlocated rabbinic academy. Other city coordinates remain unresearched here; two UNESCO points alone do not make the entire shard map-ready.
3. Samaritan traditions, Beta Israel, other African/Asian Jewish histories, detailed Sephardi/Mizrahi/Yemenite/Indian community histories, medieval legal schools, every Hasidic dynasty and contemporary conversion-status disputes require separate treatment. They are **excluded from this bounded pass**, not classified as extinct, unimportant or interchangeable.
4. The current denomination grid is strongest for Europe/North America. It is not a census of Israel or world Jewry; no unsourced adherent totals or “largest” ranking is supplied.
5. The Jewish relationship to Christianity/Islam and modern political Zionisms must be integrated with other shards. This file authorizes no claim that contemporary Jews collectively hold one position on modern states, borders or another religion.
6. Some consulted summaries compress disputed history. This file deliberately omits claims such as one fixed Zohar composition year, exact movements of medieval mystics, a universally authoritative rabbinic founding council, or an uninterrupted Sadducee→Karaite chain.
7. Access limits: BibleOdyssey emergence/Second Temple pages returned403; Israel Museum Qumran page502; YIVO article app returned a JavaScript shell; JWA Karaite article returned a bot-check shell; Sefaria pages returned a site shell without the requested text. Search snippets from these pages are **not counted as opened evidence for the display copy**. Mechon-Mamre, Josephus and the other sources below supplied the relevant bodies. MasortiUKhistory403 is separate from the opened MasortiOlami introduction. These failures do not mean the subjects themselves are inaccessible.
