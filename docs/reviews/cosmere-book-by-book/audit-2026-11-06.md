## Evidence rules

(C) means the cited evidence was opened and supports the specifically stated conclusion; it does not mean an entire book was read. Record evidence type separately: **text**, **author commentary**, **bibliography**, **repository observation**, or **inference**. (L) is an unverified lead, (U) unresolved. Neither is approved canon.

Audit each individual work, even when the UI bundles it. Record exact edition, chapter/section, object/field, source, confidence, and earliest revealing work. Publication order, in-world event order, and reader knowledge are distinct. Character beliefs and in-world scholarship are not automatically omniscient facts. Author annotations about abandoned drafts must not contaminate published canon. A second page by the same author is corroboration, not an independent witness.

A finished book audit requires a full inventory of its atlas claims, disposition of every prominent/low-confidence claim, geography and journey checks, chronology and spoiler checks, and a missing-content pass. Sampling is not completion.
